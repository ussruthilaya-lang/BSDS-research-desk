# 9. Team Communication

**9. Team communication.** The team uses a private WhatsApp group chat. Only the three team members are in it, so the instructors cannot see it. The expected response time is within 30 minutes. This is how the team already works.

To synchronise, the team meets in person or on a video call. Short questions and status updates go in the chat. Decisions that need discussion, such as a design change or a new split of tasks, are made in a meeting. The outcome is written to the project-management log in the repository.
