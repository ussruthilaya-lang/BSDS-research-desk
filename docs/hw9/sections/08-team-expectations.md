# 8. Team Expectations

**8. Team expectations.** Each member gives enough time to finish their tasks by their target dates. The guide is up to about 10 hours a week, mostly early. The build target is Sun 12/6, and Final Mastery takes place in the week of Mon 12/7. Hours can move between days and weeks to fit each person's schedule. The measure of contribution is finished tasks, not hours. Every task has an identifier, a target date and a "done when" line in the handoff file in the repository.

For unforeseen circumstances, the team follows these steps:

- **Sickness or an emergency.** The person tells the group chat as soon as they can. Each lane has a backup teammate, agreed in advance. The backup has read that lane's handoff file and takes the smallest unfinished task. The team reassigns the rest at the next meeting.
- **Interviews.** The person tells the team at least 24 hours ahead. They move their hours to another day in the same week.
- **A task that is running late.** The person says so in the chat before the target date. At the next meeting, the team decides to move the task, split it or reassign it.

For accountability, every change goes through a GitHub pull request. Another teammate reviews it before it merges. The pull requests and the project-management log in the repository record who delivered what and which decisions were made. The team meets once a week, in person or on a video call. Each member reports what is done, what is not and what is blocked.
